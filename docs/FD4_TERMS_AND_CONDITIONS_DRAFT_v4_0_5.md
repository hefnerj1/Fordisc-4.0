## 2. Nature and purpose of the Application

FORDISC 4 is an analytical tool for qualified academic, educational, research, and professional use. It compares user-entered cranial or postcranial measurements with selected reference samples and may provide classification, relationship, typicality, diagnostic, graphical, stature, and reporting outputs.

FORDISC 4 does not identify a person, determine legal identity, replace a complete biological profile, or guarantee a scientific, evidentiary, investigative, or legal conclusion. The Application is not a substitute for appropriate training, measurement proficiency, professional judgment, case-specific evaluation, peer review, laboratory policy, or applicable standards of practice.
